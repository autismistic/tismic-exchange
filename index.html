    HOW PRICES MOVE

    1. Prices update at the top of every hour while LordTismic is live. Offline, the market is closed and prices freeze.
    2. Each chatter counts once per emote per hour. Posting it 50 times counts the same as once.
    3. An emote rises when it gets a bigger share of chat's emote use than its own recent average, and falls when it gets less.
    4. Rises are capped at 10% per hour, and drops at 3%. Tier 2 rises at 2x and Tier 3 at 3x. All tiers fall at 1x.
    5. No emote drops below $5.00.
    6. Buying and selling does not move the price. Only chat does.
